How Compress works

Compress runs entirely in your browser. Your files are never uploaded, so we can't see them, keep them or lose them.

What happens to your file

  1. 01You pick a file. This page reads it on your device.
  2. 02It's compressed in the background, still on your device. Photos are resized and re-saved. Text in PDFs stays sharp and selectable.
  3. 03You download the smaller copy. Closing the tab clears everything.

Limits

Because everything runs on your device, how big a file we can handle depends on your device.

DeviceWorks wellMay be slow or stop
PhoneUp to 200 MBOver 200 MB
ComputerUp to 1 GBOver 1 GB

These are guides, not hard limits. If a file is too big, you'll see a clear message. Nothing is lost, and nothing is uploaded.

What won't shrink much

  • PDFs that are mostly text. There are no photos to make smaller.
  • Files that are already compressed. We keep the original rather than give you a bigger file.
  • Password-protected PDFs can't be opened. Remove the password first.

Privacy

  • No uploads. Your files stay on your device.
  • No accounts, no tracking, no ads.
  • Photos lose hidden details like location and camera.
  • Works offline once the page has loaded.
